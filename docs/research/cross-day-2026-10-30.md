## Forger lab check (web page)

Copying a single Canary family from the hardware Run into the Twin's Signature is enough to fool the frozen Detector on
most jobs (over-rotation on kingston and marrakesh, crosstalk on fez). J1 needs two families. This matches the stronger
Forger result: the frozen Detector leans on whichever effect is strongest. It argues for training on more cycles and for
adding Canary families, not for trusting any one effect.
